# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-12

First release.

- Russian (`ru`) registered as a selectable language with an English fallback.
- 2,444 strings translated across 58 client namespaces: chat, trajectory,
  conversation, settings, workspace and sidebar, jobs, deliverables, plans,
  goals, plugin manager, subagents and agent teams, voice input, shortcuts,
  document previews and dialogs.
- Build and maintenance tooling: English catalog extraction from the shipped
  client bundles, structural and terminology checks, offline bundle self-test.
